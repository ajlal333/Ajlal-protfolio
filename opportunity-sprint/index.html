The support problem

Your team should not rebuild the same answer for every ticket.

Support work slows down when context lives across tickets, documentation, policies, and internal systems. We define one workflow that brings those pieces together without giving AI unchecked control.

Built for a specific team

For IT services teams handling the same support work again and again.

This fits if
  • Your team handles repeated ticket types every week
  • Agents search several places before they can respond
  • Triage, routing, or drafting is slow or inconsistent
  • A support leader owns the process and its results
  • People will approve sensitive or consequential actions
This does not fit if
  • You only want a general chatbot
  • There are no repeated support tasks to study
  • We cannot review sample tickets or approved knowledge
  • You need a production platform built in two weeks
  • You want AI to act without accountable review

How it works

Four steps from a busy support queue to a build-ready plan.

The sprint designs the workflow. Building and integrating the production system is a separate project.

  1. 1

    Choose the queue

    Confirm the support team, repeated ticket types, owner, volume, and current pain.

  2. 2

    Study real tickets

    Review examples, approved knowledge, current steps, exceptions, and handoffs.

  3. 3

    Design the workflow

    Define retrieval, drafting, rules, system access, human approval, and failure handling.

  4. 4

    Hand over the pilot plan

    Deliver the chosen scope, test set, measures, controls, and build recommendation.
